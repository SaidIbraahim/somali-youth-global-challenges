## Introduction

For decades, Somali youth have displayed remarkable resilience and creativity despite systemic barriers to accessing global opportunities. This article outlines the geopolitical, financial, and infrastructural challenges Somali youth face and provides practical solutions for unlocking their full potential.


---

## Challenges Faced by Somali Youth

## 1. Digital and Business Platform Exclusion

Inaccessible platforms like PayPal, Amazon's affiliate program, YouTube Partner Program, Google AdSense, Facebook Marketplace, Spotify, Apple Services and many more.

Exclusion from global freelance markets (e.g., Upwork, Fiverr).


## 2. Barriers to International Education

Lack of local testing centers for IELTS, TOEFL, and SAT.

High costs of traveling abroad for visas and education opportunities.


## 3. Exclusion from Financial Systems

No access to PayPal, Stripe, or global cryptocurrency exchanges.

Financial barriers stifling innovation and entrepreneurship.


## 4. E-Commerce and Logistical Hurdles

Limited courier services and high shipping costs.

Challenges connecting Somali businesses to global markets.


## 5. Travel Restrictions and Representation

Limited diplomatic representation forces expensive and lengthy travel for visas.

Perceptions of insecurity limiting global engagement.



---

## Proposed Solutions

## 1. Strengthening Financial Systems

Modernize financial infrastructure to meet global compliance standards.

Collaborate with platforms like PayPal and Stripe for integration.


## 2. Expanding Diplomatic Engagement

Open embassies in key countries to simplify visa processes.

Advocate for Somalia’s inclusion in global education and financial programs.


## 3. Improving Education and Testing Access

Establish local testing centers for IELTS, TOEFL, and SAT.

Collaborate with foreign governments to create accessible scholarships.


## 4. Supporting Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Provide government-backed grants and mentorship for startups.

Encourage cryptocurrency use with proper regulation.


## 5. Creating a Stable Business Environment

Implement policies to attract foreign investment and global platforms.

Enhance security and the rule of law for international business.



---

## The Role of Somali Society

## 1. Diaspora Engagement

Advocate for Somali inclusion in global systems.

Invest in local businesses and infrastructure.


## 2. Promoting Digital Literacy

Equip youth with skills in freelancing, software development, and content creation.


## 3. Supporting Local Innovations

Develop localized solutions, such as e-commerce platforms tailored to Somalia.


## 4. Advocating for Inclusion

Engage with international organizations to highlight Somalia’s progress.



---

## Conclusion: A Call to Action

Somali youth are brimming with talent and potential, yet systemic barriers limit their global participation. Addressing these challenges through a unified approach—government reform, diaspora engagement, and local innovation—can empower them to thrive and contribute to the global digital economy.
